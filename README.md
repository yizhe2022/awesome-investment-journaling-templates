## 🧠 Methodologies for Smarter Investing
We believe in a **Hypothesis-Driven Analysis** approach. Don't just look at data; test your assumptions.

- **Write your thesis:** Why are you buying? What is the catalyst?
- **Track the process:** Focus on the quality of the decision, not just the outcome.
- **Analyze the bias:** Use our rich-text editor to capture your psychological state during market volatility.
